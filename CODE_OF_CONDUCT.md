# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is intended to be a constructive, technically focused place for collaboration. Contributors, users, and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith where reasonable, and keep discussion centered on the project and the work being proposed.

Acceptable participation includes:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking the people presenting them;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting different levels of experience and different technical approaches;
- keeping issue, pull-request, and review discussions relevant to the project.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading remarks, deliberate personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information without permission, or other conduct that makes reasonable collaboration unsafe or impractical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to this repository and its project-controlled communication spaces. It also applies when somebody is acting as an official representative of the project in another public or private space.

Technical criticism is welcome and may be direct. The requirement is that criticism remains about code, hardware behavior, documentation, testing, or design decisions rather than becoming personal abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may restrict or end participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, and whether the conduct is repeated.

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainer using a private contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. If no private contact route is available, open a public issue that contains no incident details and only requests a private contact channel.

Reports will be reviewed on a best-effort basis and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against somebody who raises a good-faith concern is itself unacceptable conduct.

## Attribution and reference

This project follows the Contributor Covenant model and uses Contributor Covenant 2.1 as its external reference for situations not addressed explicitly above. The project-specific reporting route and enforcement text in this file take precedence where they are more specific.
